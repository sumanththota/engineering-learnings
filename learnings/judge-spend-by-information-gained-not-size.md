## Principle

**Judge a step's cost by whether it produced new information, not by its size.** A step that empirically proves or disproves something is protected; a step that re-derives something already settled is the compression target.

## Why it matters

Token/time spend that reveals new information about how the system actually behaves is categorically different from spend that re-expresses information already known. A cost report can't tell these apart by size alone — you have to ask what each expensive step actually bought.
