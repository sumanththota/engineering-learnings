## Principle

**Never trust a self-report — independently re-verify.**

Re-run tests, diff the artifact, or check real state yourself rather than accepting a worker's completion claim.

## Why it matters

Self-reports can be confidently wrong with no bad intent; only checking the actual artifact catches that.
