## Principle

**Track blockers as explicit, resolvable backlog items.**

Don't let one open blocker stall the whole critical path — park it with resolution steps and keep moving on what isn't blocked.

## Why it matters

An unresolved blocker held informally (in someone's head, in chat) stalls everything adjacent to it; a tracked backlog item lets unrelated work proceed in parallel.
