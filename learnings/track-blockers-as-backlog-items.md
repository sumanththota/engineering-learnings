## Context

Migration hit several blockers mid-flight (e.g. the fixture provenance issue).

## Learning

Track blockers as explicit backlog items with resolution steps instead of stalling the critical path.

## Why it matters

A blocker parked as a defined, ownable backlog item lets the rest of the work continue; an unresolved blocker held in someone's head stalls everything touching it.
