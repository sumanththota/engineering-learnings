## Principle

**When a scale concern gets flagged up front, re-apply it at every stage of the pipeline — not just the one dial or stage you happen to be mid-way through.**

## Why it matters

A flagged concern easily gets applied to one visible dial (e.g. fewer questions) while the shape of the whole pipeline downstream goes unexamined. Nothing forces the re-check at each new stage unless it's made an explicit habit.
